3. EXPLANATION OF EOD FLAG VOLUME VS 14:30 DEVELOPMENT DATA (ITEM 2d)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why are EOD flags (~6 to 9/day) higher than the ~3/day in 14:30 development data?
1. Full-Day Volume Accumulation: At 14:30 IST, ~25-35% of daily volume remains to be traded.
   Many stocks that sit at 1.4x-1.8x at 14:30 surge past the 2.0x volume threshold during late-session institutional matching.
2. Closing Momentum Expansion: Late-session breakout moves frequently cross the +4.0% threshold between 14:30 and 15:30.
3. Broader Universe Coverage: Production EOD scan covers all EQ stocks meeting ADTV >= 5Cr, whereas development tracking
   used a restricted watchlist of ~150 symbols.
Takeaway: Retention rates and yield ratios from the 14:30 development sample are NOT applicable to EOD scans.
