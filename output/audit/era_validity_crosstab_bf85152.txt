3. ROOT-CAUSE EXPLANATION OF 2022 TAXONOMY SHIFT & DISAPPEARING CATEGORIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a. The '2022 Collapse' Explained:
   - 'Analysts/Institutional Investor Meet/Con. Call Updates' dropped to 125 in 2022 because NSE introduced
     the separate description 'Schedule of Analysts/Institutional Investor' (579 filings in 2022).
     Total Analyst Meet filings in 2022 = 125 + 579 = 704 filings (consistent with 626 in 2021 and 1,039 in 2023).
   - Similarly, 'Updates' was low in 2022 (62 filings) because NSE logged 428 filings under 'General updates'
     (lowercase 'u'). Combined total = 62 + 428 = 490 filings.
b. 'Updates' -> 'General Updates' Migration (2024–2026):
   - In late 2024, NSE standardized the exchange description 'General Updates' (capital 'U').
   - 'General Updates' went from 0 (2021-2023) -> 63 (2024) -> 492 (2025) -> 461 (2026).
   - Concurrently, 'Updates' decreased from 712 (2024) -> 264 (2025) -> 190 (2026).
c. Disappearance of 'Financial Result Updates':
   - 'Financial Result Updates' (116 in 2021, 58 in 2022, 115 in 2023, 116 in 2024, 29 in 2025, 0 in 2026).
   - Phased out as NSE enforcement required financial results to be filed strictly under 'Financial Results'
     or 'Outcome of Board Meeting' (which increased from 86 in 2024 -> 154 in 2025 -> 172 in 2026).
d. Status of Post-2024 Share Certificate Filings Drop:
   - The explanation that LODR Regulation 39(3) / RTA filings replaced standalone exchange announcements is
     explicitly marked as an INFORMED HYPOTHESIS based on observed filing patterns, NOT an authoritative SEBI citation.
e. Formal Retraction:
   - Previous claims of category 'stability' and 'zero thinning' are FORMALLY RETRACTED.
   - The historical record exhibits substantial category relabeling and regulatory volume shifts.
